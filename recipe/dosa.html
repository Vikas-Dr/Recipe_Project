Dosa Recipe

Home

Description

A crispy and delicious South Indian pancake made from fermented rice and lentil batter that is perfect for breakfast or dinner. It is typically served with coconut chutney and sambar, making it a flavorful and satisfying meal. The batter is made by soaking rice and urad dal (black gram) overnight, grinding them into a smooth paste, and allowing it to ferment for several hours. The result is a light and airy dosa that can be enjoyed plain or filled with various fillings like potatoes, cheese, or vegetables. Dosa is not only delicious but also packed with nutrients, making it a popular choice for a wholesome meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal (black gram)
  • 1/4 teaspoon fenugreek seeds (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water for soaking and grinding

Steps to Make Dosa

  1. Soak the rice and urad dal in water for a few hours.
  2. Grind the soaked ingredients into a smooth paste.
  3. Allow the batter to ferment for several hours.
  4. Heat a pan and pour a ladle of batter to make a dosa.
  5. Cook until golden brown on both sides.
