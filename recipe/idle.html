Description

Idle is a popular South Indian breakfast dish made from fermented rice and urad dal (black gram) batter. It is a soft and fluffy steamed cake that is typically served with coconut chutney and sambar. The batter is made by soaking rice and urad dal overnight, grinding them into a smooth paste, and allowing it to ferment for several hours. The result is a light and airy idli that can be enjoyed plain or with various accompaniments. Idli is not only delicious but also packed with nutrients, making it a popular choice for a wholesome meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal (black gram)
  • 1/4 teaspoon fenugreek seeds (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water for soaking and grinding

Steps to Make Idli

  1. Soak the rice and urad dal in water for a few hours.
  2. Grind the soaked ingredients into a smooth paste.
  3. Allow the batter to ferment for several hours.
  4. Grease the idli molds and pour the batter into them.
  5. Steam the idlis for about 10-15 minutes until they are cooked through.
